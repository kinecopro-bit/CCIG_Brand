Client outreach email

Subject: CCIG Is Here to Support You and Your Employees

Hi [Client Name],

Benefits questions can require significant time and resources to resolve. As your benefits partner, we’re here to help carry that workload.

The CCIG Benefits Support Center gives your employees direct access to a real person who can help them navigate benefits questions and challenges, reducing the time your team spends researching answers, contacting carriers and managing individual issues.

Our team can assist employees with:

  • Understanding plan coverage, costs, and eligibility
  • Navigating claims and carrier communication
  • Accessing member portals, forms, and resources
  • Understanding options and potential out-of-pocket costs
  • Learning more about additional benefits and programs

The result is more than a resource for employees; It’s additional support for your HR team that helps you save time and extend your internal resources.

Learn more about the Benefits Support Center: https://benefitsguides.thinkccig.com/bsc/benefit-center/
Need a HIPAA release for a specific question? Employees can complete one here: https://benefitsguides.thinkccig.com/bsc/benefit-center/hipaa-release-form
