EMPLOYEE BENEFITS · HIPAA RELEASE FORM

Some questions need your OK first.

Federal privacy law (HIPAA) keeps your health information confidential — which means your insurance carrier can’t share details about your claims, diagnoses, or records with anyone, including the CCIG Benefits Support Center, without your written permission. This release form is how you give it.

Why we need it

Your information stays protected

We only ever access your medical, dental, vision, life, or disability plan details with your explicit, written authorization — nothing is shared without it.

It lets us contact your carrier for you

Once it’s on file, our team is authorized to speak directly with your insurance carrier or provider to help resolve a claim, billing, or coverage question.

One form, on file until you say otherwise

You only need to submit it once — it stays in effect for future questions until you cancel it in writing.

Download the release form

Save the PDF, fill it out, sign and date it — then send it back to the Benefits Support Center so our team is cleared to assist you.

Please download and complete the form before you reach out

Without a signed release on file, our support team is not permitted to discuss your specific claim, coverage, or account details with your carrier on your behalf. Filling this out first is what lets us actually help.

How it works

  1. Download — use the button above to save the PDF.
  2. Fill it out — select the coverage type(s) and who we’re authorized to release information to, then add your best contact info.
  3. Sign and date it — an unsigned form can’t be used.
  4. Send it to the Benefits Support Center — attach it to your request, or bring it to your first conversation with our team.
